Hyzoom privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026 · Back to the game

Hyzoom is a free daily racing game. We collect only what the game needs to run the daily ranked race and its leaderboard. We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking services.

WHAT WE STORE ON OUR SERVER

  • Google account ID, if you sign in with Google to race ranked. This is an anonymous number Google gives us for your account. We do not receive or store your email address, profile picture or contacts.
  • The name you enter, shown publicly on the leaderboard next to your time. Do not use your real name if you prefer not to.
  • Your ranked results: finishing time, split times, which driving aids you used, and the recorded inputs of your run (used to verify the time and to let other players race your ghost after their own run).
  • Two cookies: a session cookie that keeps you signed in (up to 90 days), and an anonymous ID cookie. Both are needed for the game to work; neither is used for advertising.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Settings, your car and paint choice, and your best practice laps are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you race a ranked run.

OTHER DATA

  • Your IP address is used briefly in memory to limit abuse (rate limiting). We do not keep a record of it.
  • When you sign in, Google's sign-in button is loaded from Google, and Google's privacy policy applies to that step.
  • The game is hosted on Google Cloud.

WHO CAN SEE WHAT

Everyone can see leaderboard names and times. Players who have finished their own ranked run can race other drivers' ghosts (their recorded inputs, name and time). Nothing else is shared.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

Sign-in sessions expire after 90 days. Leaderboard results are kept so past days stay on record, unless you ask us to delete them.

DELETION AND QUESTIONS

To have your results and account ID deleted, or for any privacy question, open an issue at github.com/Dheeraj-Thota/RacingGame/issues with the name you raced under. Do not post personal details there.

CHILDREN

Hyzoom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
